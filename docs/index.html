RENT = CANVAS

What your rent buys, drawn to scale.

Every white square on this page is a studio apartment — its area is exactly the square footage a month of rent buys in that city. New York's $3,400 is a postage stamp. Tulsa's mural takes the wall. Same medium, wildly different canvas.

THE ART rent divided by local price per square foot, framed

Rent is abstract until you can hang it on a wall. This project treats monthly rent as a budget for canvas: divide the dollars by the local price per square foot and you get the size of the blank rectangle you're paying for. The bigger the rent, the smaller the canvas. The cheaper the city, the more wall you get. No photos, no floor plans — just white rectangles on a dark wall. The geometry does the arguing.

DATA & CAVEATS

Figures are indicative late-2025 asking rents for studios / one-bedrooms in central-ish neighborhoods, in USD, with rounded price-per-sqft estimates. They are meant for comparison, not for lease negotiation. Real rents vary by neighborhood, building, and mood of the market. Currencies are flattened to USD for a single wall.
